2D tracking

Home: red · Away: blue · Ball: yellow. Numbers are tracking slots, not player identities. Orientation follows ATD coordinates.
Click to seek. Shaded = the selected interval. Border = reference model (green alive, grey dead). Model rows: green = alive; Dead in grey, or by stoppage type: whistle purple, throw-in light blue, goal kick teal, corner orange, goal gold, incident red, other grey. Event row: Smart Tagging set pieces and goals (~35 s windows around the restart). Whistles row: the detected referee whistles (green; also green lines over the curves). Spectrogram (when on): the broadcast audio, 1.5-5.5 kHz, higher pitch up; a whistle is a short bright line at ~3.8 kHz. p_dead curves use each model's colour; dashed line = 0.5. Heads: p_start (cyan) and p_stop (pink) of the model chosen in Heads of, its p_dead (thin, its colour) and its decoded edges (vertical lines: solid = stoppage starts, dashed = play restarts); "m" = the decoder's margin for keeping each segment (red below 10: a close call); "w" = the part of it that comes from the whistles (whistle models only).
